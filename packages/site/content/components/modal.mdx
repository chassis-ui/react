## Examples

### Modal components

Below is a static modal example (meaning its `position` and `display` have been overridden). Included are the modal header, modal body (required for `padding`), and modal footer (optional). We ask that you include modal headers with dismiss actions whenever possible, or provide another explicit dismiss action.

<Example>
  <CxModal
    className="show d-block position-static"
    backdrop={false}
    keyboard={false}
    portal={false}
    visible
  >
    <CxModalHeader>
      <CxModalTitle>Modal title</CxModalTitle>
    </CxModalHeader>
    <CxModalBody>Modal body text goes here.</CxModalBody>
    <CxModalFooter>
      <CxButton context="secondary">Close</CxButton>
      <CxButton context="primary">Save changes</CxButton>
    </CxModalFooter>
  </CxModal>
</Example>

### Static backdrop

If you set a `backdrop` to `static`, your modal will behave as though the backdrop is static, meaning it will not close when clicking outside it. Click the button below to try it.
