## Examples

`<Popover>` wraps a trigger element as `children` and shows `content` (and an optional `title`) anchored to it on click — pass `visible` alongside `onShow`/`onHide` to control it externally instead.

<Example>
  <BasicExample client:load />
</Example>

### Four directions

Four options are available: top, right, bottom, and left aligned. Directions are mirrored when using Chassis in RTL.

</Example>
