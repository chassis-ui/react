## Overview

`TimeField` is a time of day, built on React Aria's `useTimeField`. It renders one editable segment
per unit (the hour, the minute, AM or PM), as the field of a
[Datepicker]([[docsref:/components/datepicker]]) renders a date: type a number into a segment, or
step it with the Up and Down arrow keys. Like the other form fields, it takes `label`, `help` and
the validation props, and renders a `FormLabel`, `FormHelp` and `FormFeedback` wired to it.

<Example>
  <BasicExample client:load />
</Example>

The field is a `.form-input` that holds the segments, so it takes chassis-css's sizes and states.
The segments' own styles are the date pickers', in `@chassis-ui/react/style.css` (see
[Theming]([[docsref:/getting-started/theming]])).

## Granularity and hour cycle

`granularity` sets the smallest unit: `hour`, `minute` (the default) or `second`. The locale decides
between 12 and 24 hours; `hourCycle` sets it, and `shouldForceLeadingZeros` writes `09` for 9.

## Value

A value is a `Time` from [`@internationalized/date`](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/internationalized/date/),
or a `CalendarDateTime` or `ZonedDateTime` whose date the field keeps. `onChange` receives it once
every segment has a value, and `null` once every segment is empty; a field emptied only in part
keeps its last value. With `placeholderValue`, an empty
field starts its segments from that time. Pass `value` and `onChange` to control the value, or
`defaultValue` to let the component manage it.

</Example>

## Range

`minValue` and `maxValue` bound the time. A time outside them shows the field as invalid, with its
`invalidFeedback`, and marks the segments invalid for assistive technology.

<Example>
  <RangeExample client:load />
</Example>

## Locale

The segments, their order and the AM/PM marker follow the locale: the browser's, or the one an
[`I18nProvider`]([[docsref:/getting-started/internationalization]]) sets. A right-to-left locale
keeps the time itself left to right, as it is written.

</Example>

## In a form

With `name`, the field submits its value as an ISO 8601 time, `21:30:00`, from a hidden input.
`form` links it to a form elsewhere on the page. Resetting the form restores `defaultValue`.

## States

`disabled` and `readOnly` keep the time from changing; a disabled field submits nothing. `invalid`
and `valid` color the field and show `invalidFeedback` or `validFeedback`.

<Example class="vstack gap-md">
  <StatesExample client:load />
</Example>

## Accessibility

The field is a group named by its label. Each segment is a spin button named by its unit and the
label ("hour, Start"), and Tab moves between them. Once a value is set, the group is described by
the whole time as well as by its help and feedback.
