## Smart pagination

Pass `pages`, `activePage`, and `onActivePageChange` to let `<Pagination>` render the full control automatically — Prev/Next buttons, page numbers, and ellipsis for large ranges. The Prev/Next controls default to English accessible names ("Previous"/"Next"); override them with `previousLabel`/`nextLabel` for other locales.


<Example>
  <SmartExample client:load />
</Example>

### Max visible pages

Use `maxVisiblePages` to control how many page buttons appear before collapsing to ellipsis (default is `5`).
