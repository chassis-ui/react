## Examples

`<Popover>` wraps a trigger element as `children` and shows `content` (and an optional `title`) anchored to it on click. It opens and closes itself; see [Controlling the popover](#controlling-the-popover) to drive it from outside.

### Four directions

Four options are available: top, right, bottom, and left aligned. Directions are mirrored when using Chassis in RTL.

## Controlling the popover

Pass `visible` with `onVisibleChange` to hold the open state yourself. The popover then shows and hides only when `visible` changes: a click on the trigger, a click outside and the Escape key are reported to `onVisibleChange` with the state they ask for, and nothing happens until you set it. A state setter fits as it is.

<Example>
  <ControlledExample client:load />
</Example>

`defaultVisible` opens the popover on first render and leaves it uncontrolled. `onShow` and `onHide` report that the popover has shown or hidden; they don't carry the state, so use `onVisibleChange` to control it.

## The panel

Every other attribute goes to the panel, the `.popover` element: `className`, `style`, `id`, `data-*` attributes and event handlers. A `ref` on `<Popover>` points to the panel too, and is `null` while the popover is closed. The trigger keeps its own props and ref.
