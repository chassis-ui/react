## Overview

`ContextMenu` is the region a context menu belongs to: a right-click, a long press or Shift+F10
anywhere in it opens the menu at that point, in place of the browser's own. The menu is a
[`Menu`]([[docsref:/components/menu]])'s list, `MenuList`, with the same items, headers,
dividers and submenus, so everything on that page about menu content applies here; only the trigger
and the position are different. The list is portaled out of the region and positioned with
[React Aria](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/react-aria/)'s `useOverlayPosition`, its start corner
at the pointer, and flipped above the pointer when there is no room below.

## Basic usage

Render the region's own content and a `MenuList` as the children of `ContextMenu`. The region is a
`div` by default; `component` and `asChild` change that, and `className`, `style` and the other
attributes go to it.

## Opening the menu

The menu opens on:

- **A right-click**, or Ctrl+click on macOS: the `contextmenu` event, whose default the region
  prevents so the browser's menu stays closed.
- **A long press** by a finger or a pen, after half a second without moving.
- **Shift+F10 or the context menu key** on an element inside the region, or on the region itself.
  The menu opens under that element. Nothing focusable, nothing to press the key on: give a
  region of plain content `tabIndex={0}`, as the examples on this page do.

The first item takes focus when the menu opens, so the arrow keys, Home and End move through the
items at once. A second right-click while the menu is open moves it to the new point.

Set `disabled` to leave the region to the browser: a right-click shows the browser's own menu, and
a long press and the keys do nothing.

## Closing the menu

By default a click on an item and a press outside the menu close it; both are requests to
`onVisibleChange`. A press rather than a click outside, as a native menu closes, so the release
after a long press doesn't close what the press opened. The Escape key closes the menu whatever
`autoClose` says, and so does the closing of a `Modal` or `Drawer` around the region.

`autoClose` narrows this as it does on `Menu`: `'inside'` closes only on an item, `'outside'` only
on a press outside, and `false` on neither. A form control inside the list (`MenuText` with an
input) is used in place and never closes the menu.

## Controlled

`visible` and `onVisibleChange` make the menu controlled, as on every component that opens: the
menu shows and hides only when `visible` changes, and reports each request to open or close.
`defaultVisible` opens an uncontrolled menu at first, under the region's start edge, since no
pointer has asked for it anywhere yet.

## Accessibility

- The list has `role="menu"` and is labelled by the region, the way a `Menu`'s list is labelled by
  its toggle, so a screen reader announces the region's text as the menu's name. When the region
  holds more than a name, give the `MenuList` an `aria-label` of its own instead, which replaces
  that label: `aria-label="Actions for Report.pdf"`.
- The region carries no `aria-haspopup` or `aria-expanded`: both are invalid on an element with no
  widget role, and a region is any element. A control inside the region that always opens the
  menu, such as a "more" button, is better served by a [`Menu`]([[docsref:/components/menu]]).
- Escape, and a click on an item, return focus to the element that had it when the menu opened. A
  press outside leaves focus where the press puts it.
- The keyboard needs something to focus inside the region: a row that is a link or a button, or
  `tabIndex={0}` on the region itself.
