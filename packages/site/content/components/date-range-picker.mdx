## Overview

`DateRangePicker` combines two individually-editable segmented date fields (start and end, each day/month/year) with a range-calendar popover — type both dates directly, or pick a start and end from the grid. All three stay in sync with a single selected range. The popover renders [`RangeCalendar`]([[docsref:/components/range-calendar]]), which is also available on its own wherever an inline, non-popover range calendar is needed.

## As a form field

Pass `label`/`help` to wrap the picker in a `.form-field` with an associated `FormLabel` and `FormHelp`. Pass `name` to have `DateRangePicker` create and manage a pair of hidden inputs for native form submission — `${name}Start` and `${name}End`, each an ISO 8601 date string (`2026-07-24`), with a time `2026-07-24T09:30:00` (see [Time](#time)).

## Min & max dates

Restrict the selectable range with `minValue`/`maxValue`. Dates outside the range are shown but disabled in the calendar grid. A date typed outside the range, or an end before the start, marks the field invalid, as `invalid` does, and shows `invalidFeedback` if there is one.

## Time

Pass `granularity` as `'hour'`, `'minute'` or `'second'` to add a time to both fields, as on [`DatePicker`]([[docsref:/components/datepicker#time]]): the range's dates become `CalendarDateTime`s, or `ZonedDateTime`s from a zoned `value`, `defaultValue` or `placeholderValue`. The calendar picks the days and the fields' segments edit the times. Picking a range keeps both times already set; in an empty field both take the time and zone of `placeholderValue`, else midnight with no zone. A zoned range therefore needs a zoned `placeholderValue` to stay zoned after it is cleared. Here the placeholder starts both at 3 PM.

## Unavailable dates

Pass `isDateUnavailable`, or the JSON-friendly `unavailableDates` (an array of ISO 8601 `YYYY-MM-DD` strings), to mark specific dates as unselectable without excluding them from the grid. Unavailable dates remain focusable but can't be selected — this applies to both the calendar popover and typing a date directly into either field.

## First day of week

The calendar popover's week starts on Monday by default, regardless of the active locale. Pass `firstDayOfWeek` to override it — passed straight through to the underlying [`RangeCalendar`]([[docsref:/components/range-calendar]]). See [Internationalization]([[docsref:/getting-started/internationalization]]) for how the active locale is set.

## Multiple months

Pass `visibleMonths` to show more than one month side by side in the popover, sharing a single selection — passed straight through to the underlying [`RangeCalendar`]([[docsref:/components/range-calendar]]).

## Presets

Pass `presets` — an array of `{ label, range }` entries (see [`DateRangePreset`]([[docsref:/components/range-calendar#daterangepreset]])) — to show a quick-select list next to the calendar in the popover. Selecting a preset commits its range immediately, the same as picking a start and end date from the calendar. The preset matching the current selection (if any) is shown as the active one.

## Controlling the popover

The popover manages its own open state by default. Pass `visible` (with `onVisibleChange`) to drive it from outside instead — e.g. to open the calendar from a button elsewhere on the page. `defaultVisible` sets the initial open state for the uncontrolled case, without taking over control. The earlier names `isOpen`, `onOpenChange` and `defaultOpen` still work and are deprecated.

## Form submission

Unlike `DatePicker` (one hidden input), `DateRangePicker` creates **two** hidden inputs when `name` is set: `${name}Start` and `${name}End`. This was chosen over a single combined value so each endpoint posts as an ordinary named field — no custom parsing needed on the receiving end.

## Styling

Like `DatePicker`, `DateRangePicker` isn't styled by `@chassis-ui/css`. It reuses `DatePicker`'s segmented field and popover shell, adding only a small separator between the two fields. The range-calendar grid itself is styled by [`RangeCalendar`]([[docsref:/components/range-calendar]])'s own scoped CSS. All of it is in `@chassis-ui/react/style.css`, which an app imports once (see [Theming]([[docsref:/getting-started/theming]])).

## Scope

A few things are deliberately out of scope for this pass:

- **A time picker in the popover** — the times are edited in the fields' segments, and the popover picks the days only.
- **Non-contiguous ranges** — react-aria's `allowsNonContiguousRanges` option isn't exposed.
